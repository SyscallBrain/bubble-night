<p>Bubble Night is a calm bubble shooter under the night sky. Aim, bounce off the walls and connect three or more bubbles of the same color as you travel through twelve orbits of fifty constellations.</p>

<p><b>Every level is solvable.</b> Each of the 1,800 boards ships with a solution certificate, found by an offline solver and replayed with the same engine that runs the game.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>600 levels, each designed separately for Easy, Medium and Hard</li>
<li>Daily challenge: one constellation per day and difficulty</li>
<li>Endless mode with waves that get harder, and a relaxing Zen mode with no shot limit</li>
<li>Rocks, captive stars and target-color objectives</li>
<li>Stars, streak multipliers, statistics and achievements</li>
<li>Accessibility: symbols on every bubble color, precise aim controls, full aim guide, left-handed layout, reduced motion and TalkBack board descriptions</li>
<li>Progress export and import through the system file picker</li>
<li>English and Portuguese</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.</p>
